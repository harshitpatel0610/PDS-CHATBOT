Ration Card
Generally, every family residing within the state is entitled to a ration card. For this, the head of the family member has to give complete details in the form prescribed by the state government with all require documents at the e-Gram center at the village level or at the Taluka Jan Seva Kendra or at zonal office in the urban area or he can apply online from Digital Gujarat Portal or Digital SevaSetu Portal and all family members have to do e-KYC. The Taluka Mamlatdar/Zonal Officer verifies the applicant's application and conducts spot visit where it is necessary and determines the category of the ration card, and issue ration card as per the provisions of the Citizen Charter.
A family with a ration card is eligible to receive ration under the “Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana” (PMGKAY) and Public distribution system only if the family is included under “National Food Security Act-2013”. Any member of the family can obtain entitled ration by aadhaar based authentication from any Fair Price Shop in the country or state.
Procedure for inclusion under “National Food Safety Act-2013”
(1) Identification of beneficiaries :
Socio Economic Census-2011 data has been used for identification of beneficiaries. Food, Civil Supplies and Consumer Affairs Department vide resolution dated 22/07/2014 laid down the norms for identifying “Priority Households(PHH)”. Subsequently, a decision has been taken by the state government to include more and more needy families under NFSA by making positive changes in the norms for identification of beneficiaries by resolutions dated 13/10/2020, 17/10/2020, 14/03/2023 and 22/08/2024. The process of inclusion of eligible families and exclusion of ineligible families according to these norms is an ongoing process at the taluka level.
The above eligible family should apply at the Taluka Mamlatdar Office/Zonal Office along with require documents by filling the required form.
(2)  Constitution of committee for disposal of applications:
In order to include the eligible beneficiaries who are deprived of the benefits of this Act, two-tier committee has been formed by the Government vide resolution dated 23/03/2016 and dated 21/10/2020 at the Taluka/Municipality/Corporation level and at the district level to appeal. In which the process of inclusion of needy beneficiaries under this Act is being done on regular basis after verification of applications.
The following forms are prescribed for obtaining a new ration card or for modifying an existing ration card, which are available at e-Gram center, Jan Seva Center, ATVT Center, Taluka Mamlatdar/Zonal Office or online at https://dcs-dof.gujarat.gov.in and https://digitalgujarat.gov.in or https://digitalsevasetu.gujarat.gov.in.
